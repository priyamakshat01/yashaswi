# 🎨 Professional Assets Guide for Yashaswi's Birthday Website

To make this website look like a 10/10 custom-crafted experience, here are the exact high-quality assets you can download. All the code is already configured to automatically recognize and use them the second you drop them into their folders!

---

## 📁 1. The Falling Flower Petals (For the Grand Finale Page)
* **Filename to save as:** `images/petal.png`
* **What it does:** Replaces the flat vector petals on the Flower Surprise page with a high-resolution, realistic falling photographic rose petal drifting in the wind!
* **What to search for:** "Red rose petal transparent PNG" or "Pink rose petal PNG cutout".
* **Where to get it for free:**
  - [PNGWing](https://www.pngwing.com/en/search?q=rose+petal) (Search: `rose petal transparent`)
  - [CleanPNG](https://www.cleanpng.com/free/rose-petal.html)
  - [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/images/search/rose%20petal%20png/)

---

## 🎶 3. Background Music / Song (Floating Music Button)
* **Filename to save as:** `audio/song.mp3` (or `audio/birthday-song.mp3`)
* **What it does:** When she clicks the floating 🎵 music button at the bottom-right corner, your chosen song will start playing seamlessly in the background!
* **Ideas:**
  - An acoustic instrumental version of "Happy Birthday"
  - Her favorite romantic song or acoustic lo-fi track
* **Where to get free royalty-free romantic music:**
  - [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/search/romantic/) (Search: "romantic acoustic" or "birthday acoustic")

---

## 🌹 4. High-Resolution Rose Bouquet (Optional)
* **Filename to save as:** `images/bouquet.png` or `images/rose.png`
* **What to search for:** "Red rose bouquet transparent PNG"
* **Where to get it:** [PNGWing](https://www.pngwing.com/en/search?q=rose+bouquet)

---

## ⚡ How to Add Them:
1. Download the files.
2. Rename them to the exact names listed above (e.g., `petal.png`, `photo1.jpg`, `song.mp3`).
3. Move them into:
   - `Documents/Gift website/images/` for pictures and petals
   - `Documents/Gift website/audio/` for music
4. Refresh `index.html` in your browser, and everything updates immediately!
